Next Job Radar

Independent product · Built solo · Beta, free during beta

Fewer job listings. Only the ones you can actually take.

It started with my own search for software engineering roles in Europe. Most listings failed on something basic: country of work, remote scope, language, contract. Next Job Radar checks each opening against a candidate’s real constraints first, then explains the fit with evidence from the posting.

Principles

  • Eligibility first. Hard rules come before ranking: each check returns pass, fail or unknown before any fit is assessed.
  • Fit, explained. Explanations, not scores. Feedback improves ranking without rewriting a candidate’s hard rules.
  • Honesty. Unknown stays unknown. Silence in a posting never becomes a yes.
  • Delivered as a daily email digest and a small dashboard. Users apply on the employer’s original listing.
