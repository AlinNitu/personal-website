About

I’ve always been more interested in why things break than simply knowing that they do.

That’s what drew me deeper into software engineering. From manual testing to automation architecture and designing frameworks, I’ve spent over nine years learning how complex systems behave, and how to make them more reliable.

I like solving problems and understanding how systems actually work. I usually own the automation end to end, from test strategy and framework design to CI integration, and I work closely with developers, DevOps and product along the way.

Lately that curiosity also goes into building my own products, using AI-assisted tools to get from an idea to something people can use.
