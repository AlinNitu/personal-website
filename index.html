03 — Independent product · Solo Live · early, small group of trial users

Maponim

Turns a local business's Google reviews into a one-page website its customers will recognise.

maponim.com
Real product screenshot only. No mockups.
  1. 01
    Paste a Google Maps link
    Public business info is pulled in: name, hours, phone.
  2. 02
    AI reads the reviews
    Extracts strengths, standout offerings, and what customers come back for.
  3. 03
    Publish a one-pager
    Mobile-first, editable line by line, shareable in one click.

“We don't invent claims.” The product's core constraint: every line on a site traces back to a real review, the business profile, or the owner.

Inspector · product notes
Problem spotted, product conceived, designed and built solo with AI-assisted development tools.
Grounding is a product rule, not a prompt: generated copy must be backed by source data.
English and Spanish today. Free, Pro and Agency plans; custom domains on paid tiers.
Stack: Next.js 15 (App Router) in TypeScript, React 19, Tailwind CSS 4. Supabase for database and auth; Upstash Redis for rate limiting.
AI and data: Anthropic SDK behind an LLM provider abstraction, plus a Google Places API abstraction for business lookup.
Payments and hosting: Stripe for billing; Vercel with Analytics and Speed Insights.
Tooling: Zod for validation, Vitest for tests, ESLint, lucide-react for icons.

04 — Independent product · Solo Beta · free during beta
Next Job Radar

Fewer job listings. Only the ones you can actually take.

It started with my own search for software engineering roles in Europe. Most listings failed on something basic: country of work, remote scope, language, contract. Next Job Radar checks each opening against a candidate's real constraints first, then explains the fit with evidence from the posting.

Inspector · product notes
Hard rules first, ranking second: eligibility checks return pass, fail or unknown before any fit is assessed.
Feedback improves ranking without rewriting a candidate's hard rules.
Delivered as a daily email digest and a small dashboard. Users apply on the employer's original listing.
Stack: TypeScript end to end on Node 22, in an npm workspaces monorepo. Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn/ui on Radix. PostgreSQL 18 with Drizzle ORM and SQL migrations; magic-link auth via better-auth.
Background work: a Node worker on pg-boss, a Postgres-backed job queue, polls sources, runs matching and sends digests and alerts on a schedule.
AI: Anthropic SDK with structured outputs and Zod schemas. Haiku 5.5 for job extraction and fit evaluation, Opus 5.5 for onboarding drafts. Prompt caching on the system prompt and candidate block, a per-call cost ledger and a daily budget cap.
Sources: public feeds from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Workable and Personio, careers pages with JobPosting markup, and four aggregator feeds. No LinkedIn or Indeed.
Ops: Railway hosting with two Docker images (web, worker) and managed Postgres. Resend for email with delivery webhooks. CI with Vitest, Playwright and npm audit; Dependabot; nightly backups. pino logs and operator pages for sources, costs, duplicates and traces.
